Once upon a time, in a quiet village by the river, there lived a curious little fox.
The fox loved to learn. Every morning the fox asked the wind, the trees, and the stars many questions.
Why does the river run? Why do the birds sing? Why does night follow day?
One day an old owl said, "Listen carefully, little fox. Patterns hide in every story.
If you remember what comes before, you can guess what comes after."
So the fox practiced. The fox read short tales again and again.
Once upon a time became a familiar beginning. The river, the birds, and the night became familiar friends.
And so the little fox learned that language is a chain of guesses, one token after another.
Once upon a time, the fox smiled, and began another story.
